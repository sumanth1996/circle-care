    Fieldwork Plan¶

    Notion stories: CC-2 Run elder interviews · CC-3 Run caregiver interviews · CC-4 Run guardian interviews (Sprint 15) Status: DRAFT, 2026-11-16. No sessions are booked. This plan needs a named moderator and note-taker, a reviewed consent text and a reviewed safety protocol (§5) before the first session. All times are Pacific time (PT).

    Use with the recruiting screener, the interview guides for elders, caregivers and guardians, the outreach kit and the blank recruiting tracker. The repository is public, so it holds blank templates only: the filled-in tracker, notes and synthesis sheets live in the encrypted research folder.

    1. Roles¶

    Role Does Who
    Research lead Owns the plan, the safety protocol, the restricted safety log and the decision to stop a session TBD (a person)
    Moderator Runs the conversation from the guide; runs the concept tests on the research phone TBD (a person)
    Note-taker Writes notes in the guide's note template under the participant ID; keeps the time TBD (a person). One person can moderate and take notes if the session is recorded
    Agents Prepare session sheets, turn de-identified notes into themes after each session, track saturation, draft journey maps Product team agents. They never see recordings, names, contact details or the safety log

    2. Schedule¶

    Sessions run up to 70 minutes, with at least 30 minutes after each to reset the phone and write up notes, so slots start at least 100 minutes apart. Most sessions go in the first week, because late December is hard to book.

    Days Slots (PT)
    Mon Dec 14 to Fri Dec 18 10:00 AM, 1:30 PM, 6:30 PM
    Sat Dec 19 10:00 AM, 1:30 PM
    Mon Dec 21 1:30 PM, 6:30 PM (overflow and late bookings)
    Tue Dec 22 10:00 AM, 1:30 PM, 6:30 PM (overflow and late bookings)

    Pick the slot by the participant's local time. Recruiting aims for at least 3 states, so a slot that suits one person may not suit another: 10:00 AM PT is 1:00 PM in the Eastern time zone, and 6:30 PM PT is 9:30 PM there. As a guide, offer elders a daytime slot in their own time zone, caregivers a slot between or after their shifts, and guardians who work full time an evening or Saturday slot. Record every session in the tracker in Pacific time.

    Target: the minimums (6 elders, 6 caregivers, 5 guardians) done by Sat Dec 19, with at most 3 sessions a day for one moderator. Mon Dec 14 to Sat Dec 19 has exactly 17 slots, so any cancellation spills into the overflow days unless a second moderator takes some sessions. Then:

    • Mon Dec 21, 8:00 to 10:00 AM: the affinity session for CC-5, with at least 2 people (the journey maps synthesis process). Overflow interviews after it are folded into the themes the same day.
    • Tue Dec 22 and Wed Dec 23: the 15-minute journey-map checks with participants (the synthesis kit §4). Keep them separate from any elder §6b follow-up, which needs its own call.

    3. Before each session (run sheet)¶

    • [ ] The consent form was sent at booking and the participant has had time to read it.
    • [ ] The research phone is charged, in a guest browser profile, with keyboard learning and suggestions off and the microphone blocked for the prototype site (screener §7).
    • [ ] The practice app shows the demo and Reset demo was tapped after the last session.
    • [ ] The note template for this persona is open under the participant ID (for example E03), with no name on it.
    • [ ] The recording is set to save to the encrypted research folder (if the participant agrees to recording).
    • [ ] The moderator has the safety protocol (§5) and the printed help card to hand.

    4. During and after each session¶

    1. Go through the consent outline together, including item 10 on when we may have to share something. Take a signature, or record verbal consent with the date. If the person can't consent for themselves, stop politely: we don't take proxy consent.
    2. Follow the guide. Offer a break at least every 25 minutes for elders.
    3. De-identify as you write (screener §7): "[condition]", "[med]" and "[doctor]" for health details, "[agency]" for employers and agencies, role words ("my daughter", "the aide") for names, and the state only for places.
    4. If anything in §5 comes up, pause and follow the protocol.
    5. After the session: tap Reset demo, clear the browser's site data and history for the prototype, and check the phone is back to the start.
    6. Within 24 hours: tidy the notes, check they are de-identified, save them in the encrypted folder and tick the session in the encrypted tracker.
    7. Send the incentive within 5 working days, and tick Incentive sent.
    8. Share the de-identified notes with the agents for synthesis (see the synthesis kit §1).

    5. Safety protocol for disclosures of harm (draft, needs review)¶

    Human action: the research lead reviews and approves this protocol, ideally with someone experienced in adult protective work, together with consent item 10 in the screener. Until then, no interviews run.

    This protocol covers a participant saying, or the moderator seeing, that an older adult is being neglected, abused, financially exploited, or is in an unsafe situation, or that the participant is in danger themselves. It applies whether an elder speaks about themselves, or a caregiver or guardian speaks about the person they care for.

    1. Immediate danger, in person. If someone is in danger right now (a fall with injury, a threat of violence, a medical emergency), stop the interview and call 911, or help the participant call.
    2. Immediate danger, by phone or video. A 911 call from the moderator reaches the moderator's own area, not the participant's. First help the participant, or someone with them, call 911 where they are, and stay on the line. If the moderator has to call for them, the research lead gets the address from the ID key, or asks the participant where they are if it isn't there, and gives it to the dispatcher for the participant's area.
    3. No immediate danger. Pause the interview. Thank the person for telling you. Don't investigate, ask leading questions, or promise to keep it secret, because you may need to share it (consent item 10). Don't discuss it while anyone who may be causing the harm, such as an aide or a relative in the room, can hear: offer to talk at another time instead.
    4. Offer help. Tell the participant how to reach the Adult Protective Services office in their state, and the Eldercare Locator for local services. The research lead keeps the current contact details for both on a printed help card for every session; don't rely on an app during the call.
    5. Decide whether to report. In some states anyone who suspects elder abuse must report it, not only licensed professionals, so the research team itself may have to. Before fieldwork, the research lead checks the rules for every state that participants are booked from. After a disclosure, the research lead decides and makes any report. Don't report from the session itself unless the danger is immediate.
    6. Carry on or stop. Ask whether the participant wants to continue, take a break or stop. They receive the incentive either way.
    7. Record. Write the details, and the fact that the protocol was used, only in the research lead's separate, restricted safety log, never in the notes, the tracker or the repository.
    8. Debrief. The moderator and the research lead talk within 24 hours. The moderator can step back from later sessions.

    6. Storage and deletion¶

    From screener §7, with dates:

    What Where Deleted
    Name and contact (ID key) One encrypted, access-restricted file Once incentives are paid and follow-ups are done
    Recordings Encrypted research folder Within 30 days of transcription
    De-identified notes Encrypted research folder, one file per ID Within 12 months; log the deletion (date and ID only) in the encrypted tracker
    Filled-in tracker and synthesis sheets Encrypted research folder, copied from the blank templates in this repository With the last de-identified notes; the tracker's deletion log (ID and date only) is kept until then
    Safety log The research lead's restricted file As the research lead's reviewed guidance says

    Nothing with a name, contact detail, recording or raw note goes into this repository, Notion or Slack, and neither does a filled-in tracker or synthesis sheet.
