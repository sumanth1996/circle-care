    Outreach Kit¶

    Notion story: CC-1 Recruit and screen interview participants (Sprint 15 prep) Status: DRAFT, 2026-11-16. No one has been contacted. Every message below is a template for the recruiting owner to adapt and send. Agents do not send outreach, contact participants or pay incentives.

    Use with the recruiting screener. Fill the placeholders in square brackets before sending. Never add a participant's name, phone number or email to this file or to the repository: contact details live only in the encrypted ID key (screener §7).

    1. Timeline (Pacific time)¶

    Dates What Owner
    Mon Nov 16 to Fri Nov 20 Name the recruiting owner, secure the incentive budget, get the consent text reviewed, review and approve the safety protocol (see the fieldwork plan §5) PO and recruiting owner
    Mon Nov 23 to Fri Dec 4 Send outreach to each channel in screener §3; screen people as they reply; send consent forms. Thanksgiving week is slow, so send the first outreach on Mon Nov 23 Recruiting owner
    Mon Dec 7 to Fri Dec 11 Fill gaps in the quotas; confirm every booking; send reminders Recruiting owner
    Mon Dec 14 to Tue Dec 22 Interviews (see the fieldwork plan) Moderator and note-taker
    Tue Dec 22 and Wed Dec 23 15-minute follow-up calls to check the journey maps Moderator

    Recruiting stops early for a segment once its minimum is booked and the mix in screener §2 is met.

    2. Email to an Area Agency on Aging or senior center¶

    Subject: Short paid conversations with older adults about getting help at home

    Hi [coordinator's name],

    I'm [owner's name], working on Circle Care, a concept app that helps an older person, their caregivers and their family share medicine schedules and check-ins while the older person stays in charge of their own information.

    Before we build further, we want to hear from older adults who live at home and get some help. Conversations are up to 70 minutes with breaks, by phone, video or in person, between December 14 and 22. Each person gets a [$40] gift card, by mail or email.

    Could you share the attached one-page flyer, or mention it in a newsletter? We never ask you for anyone's contact details: people who are interested contact us directly. The consent outline is attached so you can see how we keep and delete notes.

    Thank you, [owner's name]

    3. Message to a home-care agency owner or manager¶

    Subject: Paid conversations with caregivers about shifts and hand-offs

    Hi [manager's name],

    I'm [owner's name], working on Circle Care, a concept app for shift hand-offs, medicine schedules and check-ins between paid caregivers and families.

    We'd like to talk with caregivers about a recent shift: what they had to remember, how they handed over and what got in the way. Conversations are up to 70 minutes, outside work hours, between December 14 and 22, and each person gets a [$40] gift card.

    With your permission, could we post the attached flyer in the break room or staff chat? Your agency won't see anyone's answers, and we won't recruit through supervisors. If your policy needs written approval, a caregiver can ask us for a letter to give you; you'll then know only that they took part.

    Thank you, [owner's name]

    4. Post for a caregiver support group (ask the facilitator first)¶

    Are you a legal guardian or hold power of attorney for an older adult? We're designing a care app called Circle Care and want to learn how you keep track of someone's care from a distance: what you need to know, what you need to approve, and what wears you out. Up to 70 minutes by phone or video, December 14 to 22, with a [$40] gift card as thanks. Reply to [project email] or call [project phone]. We keep your answers under a number, not your name.

    5. Large-type flyer (print at 16 point or larger)¶

    Do you get some help at home?

    We'd like to hear about your day.

    A friendly conversation of up to 70 minutes, with breaks.

    By phone, video or in person, December 14 to 22.

    You'll receive a [$40] gift card to thank you.

    You can stop at any time and still receive it.

    Call or write to [project phone] / [project email]

    Circle Care is a design project. We do not sell anything, and we keep your answers under a number, not your name.

    Use tear-off tabs with the project phone or email only. Never put a participant's details on a flyer.

    6. Phone screening script¶

    1. "Thank you for calling about the Circle Care conversations. Do you have about five minutes for a few questions to see if it's a good fit?"
    2. Ask screener §4.1, then the segment questions in §4.2, §4.3 or §4.4. Read questions aloud for older adults and go at their pace.
    3. If they qualify: "Thank you, you'd be a great fit. Can I send you the consent form to read before we pick a time?" Agree the format (phone, video or in person) and two possible times from the fieldwork plan §2.
    4. If they don't qualify: "Thank you so much for calling. We don't need more people like you for this round, so I'll delete your details today."
    5. Close every call with the line in screener §4: "We keep your answers under a number, not your name. We don't need to know about your health, and if you tell us something we don't need, we won't write it down."
    6. Record the result under the participant ID only, in the encrypted copy of the recruiting tracker, never in the repository.

    7. Confirmation and reminder¶

    Confirmation (sent when booked): Thank you for agreeing to talk with us. Your conversation is on [day, date] at [time in your time zone], by [phone / video / in person at …]. It lasts up to 70 minutes and you can take breaks. Please read the consent form before we talk; we'll go over it together at the start. If you need to change the time, reply or call [project phone].

    Reminder (sent the day before): A reminder that we're talking tomorrow, [day] at [time in your time zone]. If something has come up, just let us know and we'll find another time.

    Send times in the participant's own time zone. The tracker records every session time in Pacific time.
