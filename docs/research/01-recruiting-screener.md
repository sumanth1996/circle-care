# Recruiting Plan and Screener

Notion story: CC-1 Recruit and screen interview participants
Status: DRAFT / TEMPLATE. No participants have been recruited or contacted. Every step below needs a human to run it.

> Circle Care is a CSPO-training concept product. This plan describes how discovery interviews *would* be run. Nothing here reports real research.

## 1. Goal

Recruit enough people across the three personas to test whether the core problems Circle Care is built around are real, frequent and painful:

- **Elders** (like Margaret "Maggie" Lee): staying independent while accepting help, and keeping control over who knows what.
- **Professional caregivers** (like Ana Rivera): proving work was done, and hand-offs between shifts.
- **Legal guardians / healthcare POA holders** (like Sarah Lee): knowing Mom is OK without calling five times a day, and making decisions with good information.

Secondary: family helpers (like grandson James) and respite caregivers (like Ruth Okafor). Interview 1–2 of each if they come up naturally, but they do not count toward quotas.

## 2. Quotas

| Segment | Minimum | Stretch | Mix to aim for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elders (70+, living at home) | 6 | 8 | At least 2 living alone; at least 2 who use a smartphone only for calls/texts; at least 1 with low vision or hearing |
| Professional caregivers | 6 | 8 | At least 3 agency-employed, at least 2 private-pay/independent; at least 2 non-native English speakers |
| Legal guardians / POA holders | 5 | 7 | At least 2 long-distance (more than 1 hour away); at least 1 court-appointed guardian (not POA); at least 1 who also works full time |
| Family helpers (bonus) | 0 | 2 | — |

Stop recruiting a segment when two consecutive interviews produce no new themes (saturation), but never below the minimum.

Where possible, recruit **linked triads** (an elder, their caregiver and their guardian) — at least 2 triads — so we can compare how each person describes the same situation. Interview each person separately.

## 4. Screener

Ask by phone or a short form. Read questions aloud for elders. Record answers in the tracker using a participant ID only.

### 4.1 All segments

1. Which best describes you? (Older adult who gets some help at home / Paid caregiver / Legal guardian or power of attorney for an older adult / Family member who helps / Other) — *route to segment*
2. What state do you live in? — *aim for at least 3 states*
3. Are you, or anyone in your household, employed by a company that makes health or caregiving apps? — *exclude if yes*
4. Have you taken part in a paid research interview in the last 6 months? — *exclude if 2+*
5. Are you comfortable with a 45–60 minute conversation by phone, video or in person? Which do you prefer?

### 4.2 Elders

| # | Question | Qualify if |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | How old are you? | 70+ (65–69 OK if receiving regular help) |
| E2 | Where do you live? (own home, with family, assisted living, nursing home) | Own home or with family |
| E3 | Does anyone help you with things like medicines, meals, appointments or errands? How often? | At least weekly |
| E4 | How many medicines do you take on a regular schedule? | 2+ |
| E5 | What kind of phone do you use, and what do you use it for? | Any (record for mix) |
| E6 | Is there anything that makes phone screens or calls harder for you? (eyesight, hearing, hands) | Any (record for mix) |
| E7 | Are you able to agree to take part yourself today? | Yes — **if the person cannot give their own consent, do not interview them** |

### 4.3 Professional caregivers

| # | Question | Qualify if |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Do you currently provide paid in-home care to an older adult? | Yes |

|---|---|---|

| G3 | How far do you live from them? | Record for mix |
| G4 | Who else helps care for them? | At least one other person (family or paid) |

## 5. Consent form outline (needs review before use)

Plain language, 14pt+ print, read aloud for elders. Draft sections:

1. Who we are and that Circle Care is a student/concept project, not a company selling anything.
2. What we will ask about (daily routines, help, privacy) and how long it takes.
3. Taking part is voluntary; you can skip any question or stop at any time and still receive the incentive.
4. Recording: audio only, with separate opt-in; notes taken either way.
5. **Please do not tell us specific diagnoses, medicine names or doctors' names.** If you do, we will remove them from notes.
6. How data is stored, who sees it (only the research team), and when it is deleted (recordings within 30 days of transcription; de-identified notes within 12 months).
7. Quotes may be used without names or identifying details.
8. Contact for questions or withdrawal.
9. Signature or recorded verbal consent with date.

**Human action:** have the consent text reviewed (e.g., by an instructor or IRB-style reviewer) before first use. Elders who cannot consent for themselves are excluded; we do not take proxy consent for research.

## 6. Incentive

- Proposed: $40 gift card for 45–60 minutes; $20 for a 15-minute follow-up.
- Offer the choice of a physical card mailed or e-card; some elders will not want an e-card.
- Budget for minimum quotas: 17 × $40 = $680 (+ 20% buffer). **Funding not yet secured.**

## 7. De-identification rules

- Assign IDs at screening: `E01…`, `C01…`, `G01…`, `F01…`; triads share a suffix (e.g., `E03-T1`, `C02-T1`, `G04-T1`).
- Keep the name ↔ ID key in one access-restricted file, separate from notes. Delete it after incentives are paid and follow-ups are done.
- In notes: replace names with role words ("my daughter", "the aide"); replace medicine names with "[med]"; replace conditions with "[condition]"; replace employer/agency names with "[agency]"; generalize location to state.
- Never paste notes into tools that train on inputs.
- Recordings: store encrypted, delete within 30 days after transcription.
