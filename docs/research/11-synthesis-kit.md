This adds two things to the synthesis process in the [journey maps](05-journey-maps.md#synthesis-process-after-interviews): a rolling check after every session, so saturation shows up while interviews are still running, and the script for checking the finished maps with 5 participants, which CC-5 needs.

## 1. What agents may receive

Agents work only from notes that the note-taker has already de-identified (the [fieldwork plan](10-fieldwork-plan.md) §4). Before sharing a note, the note-taker checks that it has:

- the participant ID only, with no name, initials, address, phone, email, employer or agency name;
- "[condition]", "[med]" and "[doctor]" in place of any health detail the participant volunteered;
- no recording, transcript file or photo.

If an agent finds anything identifying in a note, it stops work on that note, doesn't repeat or store the detail, and tells the research lead which ID it was in.

## 4. Checking the journey maps with participants (CC-5)

CC-5 needs the three journey maps checked with at least 5 participants, including at least one from each segment. Use a 15-minute follow-up call (incentive in screener §6) on Tue Dec 22 or Wed Dec 23, after the affinity session on Mon Dec 21 (the [fieldwork plan](10-fieldwork-plan.md) §2). Keep it separate from any elder §6b follow-up, which needs its own call.

**Script.**

1. "Last week you told us about [their situation, in general words]. We've drawn a picture of a typical day for people like you. Can I read it to you and you tell me what's right and what's wrong?"
2. Read each stage of the map for their segment, one at a time. After each: "Does this sound like your day? What would you change?"
3. "Is there a moment that matters to you that we've left out?"
4. "Which part of this day would you most like to be easier?"
5. Thank them and confirm the follow-up incentive.

**Record** for each stage: confirmed, changed (with the change) or wrong. If any checker from a segment calls a stage of that segment's map wrong, the stage is reviewed against the notes and rewritten or removed before the maps change status to "Evidence-based, v1".
