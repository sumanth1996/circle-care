    FAQ¶

    About the product¶

    What is Circle Care, in one sentence?

    A care-coordination app that keeps an older adult, their caregivers and their legal guardian on the same daily plan, with the older adult in charge of who sees what.

    Is Circle Care a real, available product?

    No. It is a concept product built by Sumanth as a Certified Scrum Product Owner (CSPO) training assignment and portfolio piece. The working prototype demonstrates the experience; it is not offered to the public and must not be used with real health information.

    Who is it for?

    Older adults who live at home and want help without losing independence; the professional and family caregivers who support them; and the legal guardian or healthcare power of attorney who makes decisions with them. See Personas.

    How is it different from a medication reminder app or a family group chat?

    It combines the pieces that usually live in separate tools — medicine schedules, caregiver shifts, check-ins, emergencies and decisions — around one person, and gives that person real control: a Privacy Center, an access log, one-tap pause, and visibility of every decision. Caregivers see care details only during their shifts. The competitive teardown frames the positioning to be validated.

    What is the North Star metric?

    Verified care tasks completed on time per week. In the prototype that is doses recorded as taken within 60 minutes of their scheduled time, plus completed care tasks, over the last 7 days. It is shown on the guardian's home screen and weekly digest.

    About the prototype¶

    Is any of the data real?

    No. Maggie, Sarah, Ana, James and Ruth are fictional. Phone numbers use the reserved 555-01xx range. Health readings are generated by a formula. Nothing leaves your browser.

    Where is the data stored?

    In your browser's local storage, encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a key that the browser will not let any script read or export. Each browser has its own separate copy. See Security & privacy.

    What is the MFA code?

    482913 — for every sign-in and for the sign-up flow. You can also tap the code on screen to fill it in.

    Do notifications actually get sent?

    No. The prototype shows in-app banners and an Alerts list. Real push, SMS and email delivery is planned for production.

    Does the app use my location, camera or microphone?

    Location: no — check-in shows the flow but does not read GPS. Microphone: only if you tap Answer with my voice on a reminder and your browser can recognise speech on the device; if recognition would need a cloud service, the feature stays off. Camera: no.

    Why are some photos blank?

    Stock photos are loaded from Unsplash and need internet access. Without it, they show as soft placeholders or coloured circles.

    Can I install it on my phone?

    Yes. Open the deployed URL in Safari (iOS) or Chrome (Android) and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen and works offline after the first visit.

    How do I reset everything?

    Demo · Name → Reset demo. To also destroy the encryption key and uploaded files, use Privacy Center → Close this circle and delete its data as Maggie or Sarah.

    Privacy and compliance¶

    Is Circle Care HIPAA compliant?

    The prototype is designed to the HIPAA Privacy, Security and Breach Notification Rules, but it is not a production system and has not been assessed. Whether HIPAA legally applies depends on the business model: a consumer app families use directly is usually covered by the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule and state privacy laws instead; selling to home care agencies would make Circle Care a business associate. See the regulatory memo and HIPAA risk analysis.

    Who can see Maggie's medicines?

    Maggie, Sarah (her verified guardian), family members in her circle, and professional caregivers only while checked in to a shift. When Maggie pauses sharing, family and caregivers lose access to everything except SOS, the emergency card and the circle list. See the permission matrix.

    Why does the guardian keep access when Maggie pauses sharing?

    A healthcare power of attorney carries legal authority. The guardian keeps access but is told sharing is paused. Health readings are the exception: Maggie can stop sharing those with Sarah separately.

    Can Maggie remove her guardian from the circle?

    Not in the app. The Privacy Center explains that Sarah's access can only be changed through the legal process. Maggie can remove anyone else.

    Is Circle Care a medical device or an emergency service?

    No. The Health page and SOS screens say so explicitly: "Circle Care isn't a medical device; call 911 in an emergency" and "It is not a monitored emergency service — call 911 if you are in danger."

    About this wiki¶

    What happens when the wiki and the code disagree?

    The code wins. Raise a change to the wiki (see How to contribute).

    Where are the user stories?

    In the Notion Circle Care — Product Hub. Story IDs (CC-n) appear in code comments and throughout this wiki; the traceability matrix maps them to pages, files and tests.
