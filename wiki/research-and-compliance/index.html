    Research & Compliance¶

    Working drafts for the backlog stories that need real people, counsel or vendors to complete: user research, regulatory and data-protection analysis, security planning and the pilot plan. They live in docs/research, docs/compliance, docs/security and docs/pilot and are indexed for GitHub readers in docs/README.md.

    Drafts — not legal advice

    Status as of 2026-10-19: working drafts. Circle Care is a CSPO-training concept product; all people in the prototype are fictional. Nothing in these documents reports real interviews, signed contracts, legal review or completed external tests unless it says so explicitly. Compliance documents are not legal advice. Each document starts with its Notion story ID and lists the human action still needed.

    Open findings¶

    Findings that came from reading the code while writing these documents and this wiki. They are candidates for backlog stories.

    # Finding Status Where discussed
    1 Several store actions bypassed the permission check Fixed Oct 19, 2026 (new handoffs resource, actor checks) Permission matrix — coverage gaps
    2 The elder cannot read guardian-only notes (notes.private), nor hand-offs; the Notes hint says she can Open — product decision Notes & documents
    3 A <meta> CSP cannot block framing; clickjacking protection needs an HTTP header Open — production Security & privacy
    4 No committed axe-core or Playwright scripts Fixed — 29 scenarios, 24 screens Quality & testing
    5 The /pilot form collects name and contact but discards them; production needs a separate, deletable contact store Open Pilot & feedback
    6 An unverified guardian is not downgraded to family access, although the Care circle page says so Open Care circle & access
    7 The guardian has no way to remove a member, although the suspicious-access alert tells her to do so in Care circle Open Care circle & access
    8 The elder and caregiver apps have no calendar screen, although the appointment form says Maggie sees every appointment Open Care calendar
    9 Closing a circle in the prototype also discards the access log, although the UI says it is kept six years Open — production design Privacy Center
    10 Maggie's age shows as 81 (website, emergency card) and 80 (birth year 1946, Health); SOS responder shows a non-San Francisco demo address Fixed in v0.4 Release notes v0.4
    11 docs/security/permission-matrix.md lacks the handoffs, vitals and appointments rows and still cites 180 tests Open — regenerate the doc Security & privacy

    Conventions¶

    • Story IDs match the Notion backlog (CC-n).
    • Hypotheses are labelled H-E (elder), H-C (caregiver), H-G (guardian).
    • "Demo" = demonstrated in the browser prototype with fictional data; "Prod" = production work not yet built.
    Circle Care is a concept product. All people and data in it are fictional.
